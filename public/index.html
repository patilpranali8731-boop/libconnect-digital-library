ABOUT LIBCONNECT

Bringing Knowledge Closer to the Community.

LibConnect is a digital library platform designed for community centres. It provides convenient access to educational and informational resources for students, learners and community members.

Our goal is to make learning resources accessible regardless of whether someone has access to a traditional library.
